# Functor Vision

> Vision documents describing the long-term aspirations, principles, and purpose of the Functor ecosystem.

This directory contains documents that guide the direction of Functor.

Unlike RFCs, vision documents are not normative specifications.

Unlike implementation documentation, they do not define architectural requirements or implementation details.

Instead, they explore:

- Why Functor exists
- What problems it seeks to address
- How humans and intelligences may collaborate
- The principles that should guide future decisions
- The long-term future Functor hopes to enable

These documents should be viewed as sources of inspiration and strategic direction.

---

# Relationship to the Rest of the Repository

The repository is organized into three major layers.

```text
vision/
    Why Functor exists

rfcs/
    What Functor is

docs/
    How Functor is implemented
```

The relationship between them can be visualized as:

```text
Vision
    ↓ inspires

RFCs
    ↓ specify

Implementation
```

Vision documents may influence future RFCs.

RFCs may influence future implementations.

The flow is directional but not prescriptive.

# Interpretation

Vision documents should not be interpreted as implementation requirements.

For example:

```text
A vision document may inspire an RFC.

An RFC may require an implementation.

The reverse is not necessarily true.
```

Not every idea discussed in vision documents requires software representation.

Not every vision statement should become a specification.

Vision documents are intended to encourage exploration while RFCs define concrete commitments.

---

# Evolution

Vision documents may evolve over time.

Unlike RFCs, they are not expected to remain stable specifications.

As understanding grows, these documents may be revised to better reflect the goals, values, and aspirations of the Functor ecosystem.

The purpose of revision is not to preserve wording.

The purpose is to preserve intent.

---

# Closing Thought

Functor began as an exploration of tools, notebooks, and computation.

It has gradually evolved into a broader exploration of how understanding itself may be represented, validated, shared, and evolved.

These documents capture that ongoing exploration.

They describe not what Functor is today, but what it may become.
